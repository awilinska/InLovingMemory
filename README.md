# IN LOVING MEMORY
## Vertical slice

A vertical slice of a game depicting reality from the perspective of a caregiver for a loved one with Alzheimer's. 

The most important element of the vertical slice is the dynamic camera system—the camera moves when the player feels overwhelmed by how the person with Alzheimer’s perceives the world, symbolizing how reality is obscured by illusions and delusions, as well as the slow loss of one’s sense of self even when not afflicted by the disease.

I created most of the models myself in Blender (some of them, such as the characters, are from the Unity Asset Store). I used a script that generates outlines along the edges to give the whole thing a more hand-drawn, comic-book style.
